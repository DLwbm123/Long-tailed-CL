# Why inspect orientation after R77

R67–R75 sampling primary routes did not establish stable gains over their strong common SFT. D76 found stage-dependent geometry changes, not a universal within-class variance explanation. R77 retains a small positive conditional increment over its same distribution readout SFT, while final/tail remain below original SFT and full-support inference costs more. The post-R77 RKHS norm audit exposes intertwined count/geometry shifts; it does not justify a prior/offset rescue.

The native R62–R77 pipeline explicitly excludes image augmentation and repeatedly adapts to the same deterministic image view. None of D76 geometry, R77 support distributions, or pair-kernel norms measures whether the same endpoint maps a fixed alternative image orientation to different class evidence. This is a distinct input-level representation question, directly relevant to a future medically appropriate view-robust adaptation mechanism, rather than another weight/score/module change. Missing sensitivity is not itself proof of harmful orientation dependence.

Primary reference checked: Guo et al., Improving image classification of gastrointestinal endoscopy using curriculum self-supervised learning, Scientific Reports14,6100(2024), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-53955-8 . Their pipeline includes horizontal/vertical flips and other transforms on a MODIFIED16-class HyperKvasir setup with SSL/unlabeled data. This is precedent for considering orientation, not validation that a horizontal flip is label preserving for this fixed23-class split, not reproduction of their method, and not a reason to import their entire augmentation recipe. Existing five designated PDF notes remain unchanged; no full audit repeated.

A single fixed horizontal flip is used descriptively across all six stages and all three sealed endpoints, not searched or selected by accuracy. No new representation is trained and no SFT comparator is optimized. Counts/probability changes on reused TRAIN cannot establish patient robustness or predict VAL benefit. Image overlays, device artifacts, anatomical conventions or crop interactions may contribute, so even large disagreement cannot identify a unique cause. Do not use low sensitivity as a pilot veto or high sensitivity as automatic authorization for a new loss. Later experimental mechanism must remain falsifiable with unchanged old scientific gates.
