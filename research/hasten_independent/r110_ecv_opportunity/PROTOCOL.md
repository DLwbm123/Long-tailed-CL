## Legal reference and roots

No skipped or unfavorable slot is replaced. Scenario seed 75110+100*stage+index is fixed. Two continuation tapes 31001/31002 are paired across all candidates, never selected after outcomes.

Donor training may use all current TRAIN, but its model/optimizer never initializes a diagnostic root. At a selected real snapshot, two S-supported CURRENT classes chosen by the scenario RNG become pseudo future arrivals. All other S-supported classes remain in the prefix, grouped by their original birth stages. Both kinds are transient conditioned-on-snapshot evaluation evidence, not a deployable extra historical query bank.

## Fixed candidate pool, at most 16 unique sets

P1 uses two original ZERO/BAL anchors; per anchor risk edits of1/2/4 tokens, one4-token coverage edit and one2-token random edit (12 total slots with anchors), followed by one full geometric reconstruction and one matched random reconstruction per anchor (four slots). Thus raw-delta and the second local-random slot of v1 are replaced by diagnostic structural/matched-random slots. This is a P1 candidate-space diagnostic, not an unreported claim about the complete original v1 matrix.

Every set has the same token/byte budget and retains common centroid-nearest coverage globals and all required global parents. Every edit/reconstruction inherits its anchor's per-class global/local counts. Structural reconstruction selects feasible farthest global coverage then balanced novel locals. Matched random reconstruction matches, for every class and global/local kind, both selected count and overlap with the anchor: exact modification magnitude. It has64 complete attempts per class, no reward input. Missing matches and duplicate/no-op sets are reported, never fabricated. Different anchors can have different quotas. Large structural changes are not described as four-token local edits. Candidate generation sees no query label or return.

## Query sizes, feedback and fixed gates

Each role's LARGE query uses all currently legal role evidence with both FIT/AUDIT present and S support. SMALL is the first at most2 components/class under a fixed scenario/class shuffle, nested in LARGE. The same supported evaluation class set E is used for small/large and candidates at each horizon; different E across horizons is allowed and reported. Support, training trajectories, and candidate sets never change when query size changes. A single forward on the combined legal query yields both roles/sizes; no extra optimizer or image encoding is spent for larger scoring.

Reward is supplied-method macro BA change relative to same-root ZERO shadow averaged over horizons minus2*max_h[-old-tail BA change-.005]_+. Old tail is fixed at the root using already observed TRAIN counts<250 and classes older than the real current stage. Missing old-tail query is not zero harm and cannot satisfy the risk gate. Units are 0–1 accuracy, .005=0.5pp. H1 and H3 are both saved. CE/Brier do not enter this reward. Identical reference gives exact paired zero.

Select by FIT argmax after averaging both predeclared tapes; ties prefer ZERO. Report small/large FIT selections on the SAME large AUDIT, plus the full2x2 selection/scoring matrix. AUDIT never selects/trains/deploys; its self-argmax is descriptive finite-candidate opportunity only. No changes to this protocol after outcomes. Root stage/scenario, class counts/denominators, nontrivial candidate counts, matching failures, compression pressure, H1/H3 differences, and per-component rescue/harm are retained. Before final scientific delivery, saved-only verification and maximum single-component/leave-one-component influence analysis are required; removal that empties a class is inestimable rather than zero-filled.

Per-stream development-evidence gate: all16 slots evaluable; LARGE FIT→LARGE AUDIT mean R>=.005; at least8 positive scenario means; at least8 old-tail-evaluable scenarios and mean old-tail change>=-.005. Both streams required. These are prospective development effect/coverage gates, not significance or patient-independence guarantees. Failure or inconclusiveness is reported completely; no threshold, candidate, seed or budget rescue. This P1 run never automatically starts P2/RL even if evidence passes.
