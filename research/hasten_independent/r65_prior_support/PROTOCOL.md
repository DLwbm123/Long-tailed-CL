All other R63 architecture/data/folds/schedule are retained. This is not merely changing rank/lr/epoch, and it is not a claim to invent Balanced Softmax.

Source: Ren et al., Balanced Meta-Softmax, NeurIPS2020, Eq3–4: https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/file/2ba61cc3a8f44143e1f2f13b2b729ab3-Paper.pdf . Use only its fixed-prior likelihood principle; no meta sampler/meta validation. Under equal p(x|class) and different class priors, training posterior is softmax(s+log n) when uniform-prior posterior is softmax(s). Medical conditional shift and finite-sample support error may violate this explanatory model. Inverse weighting and this likelihood can target the same ideal posterior in a flexible population model; differences here are finite-sample optimization/regularization, not proof that R63's target was mathematically invalid. Per-query logit gradient is bounded without the N/(C*n_y) multiplier, but this does not prove overall variance or accuracy improves.

Falsifier: unchanged BOTH adoption gate fails, rare-query development does not improve despite lower amplification, or natural training favors heads and worsens tails. Retain all negative results.
