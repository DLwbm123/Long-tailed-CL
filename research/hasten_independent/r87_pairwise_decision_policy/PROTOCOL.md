Frozen before new resource probes/software/native/training. ## Motivation and falsifiable hypothesis
RLAP-CLIP suggests binding a learned action to the deployed classifier rather than only indirect allocation; its RLPO formulation and conditional ablations do not establish necessity of GRPO. R82 identified an ineffective tiny-class actuator; R83 repaired parameter actuation without reliable decision gain; R84-R86 show limited supervised/information gains. New question: can an explicitly budgeted decision policy use group-relative optimization to generalize protected macro-accuracy corrections better than the SAME policy trained by supervised CE or an exact reward gradient?

The new freedom is decision arbitration, not exemplar weighting: even one S example admits a nontrivial action if its class is a runner-up. It cannot correct a true class ranked below2. This is a prospective structural limitation, not permission for top-k, threshold, margin, feature, semantic, kernel or temperature search. D50 direct visual-gradient feedback and R59 learned angular metric are not resumed.

## Data and action tree
Reuse six sealed R84/R81 states, existing saved raw NCM logits, S prototypes/counts and exact R82/R84 Q/Qa/Qb IDs. No new image/feature encoding, classifier dot-products, visual gradients/optimizers, patch, prompts, text or data. Access each stage chronologically, arrived classes only. Q has been used in historical development and source SFT: this is not fresh confirmation.

For every sample its original top1/top2 class pair is fixed, ties resolved by class-index stable sort. A directed action (target c, original winner d) swaps only those two logits on samples originally ranked d then c. Other logits/samples are unchanged. Exactly tied top scores remain unchanged by a swap and are not counted as affected rows. Legal actions are stop plus directed pairs present in the current UNLABELLED query-score slice. Exactly two decision slots: stop terminates; a nonzero first action excludes BOTH directions of that unordered pair from slot2, with stop still legal. No dynamic top2 recomputation. Distinct pair groups are disjoint in samples; changes in counts/CE add, but the positive-part protected damage can couple actions through class totals. Two slots are a fixed correction budget, not tuned horizon. No Qb labels in state/masks.

Per-action11 features: log S count target/source divided by log1000; class ages/5; S-prototype cosine; affected unlabelled-row fraction; mean/std raw logit gap divided by20; mean original target/source probabilities; stop indicator. Concatenate global action-feature mean (11), slot indicator (1), four indicators relating current target/source to previous target/source (4). Shared27->32tanh->32tanh->1 MLP, no class-ID embeddings; zero final head gives uniform initial action probabilities. Autoregressive categorical policy, not a generative VLM. Features and finite legal masks are identical across all arms. Evaluation state uses unlabelled evaluation-slice logits, so the classifier is episode-transductive; disclose this for every arm.
