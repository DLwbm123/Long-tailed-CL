## Question and evidence
R88 complete reconstruction improves overall and macro accuracy against matched loss/weight ablations, while final tail benefit is only ~0.03pp and falls below bounded NCM. Old task prompts are frozen, but shared experts/attention/policy continue changing. Ordered class0 VAL recall moves from95.15% at stage4 to4.24% at stage5. This motivates (but does not prove) shared-function drift; no additional old-endpoint scan is used. Separate two causes: unequal per-class classification-risk mass, and failure to preserve previous-stage decisions under new-class competition. R89 measures their individual and interaction effects. It is not a GRPO-specific claim.

## Exact objectives
Let T_s be the actual training rows at stage s and n its number of classes. N_sc counts only T_s (old retained counts and new full class counts). MACRO/JOINT multiply each classification contribution (1+d_y)*CE by |T_s|/(n*N_sy). This is an unbiased row-sampling estimator of a class-mean difficulty-weighted CE; contrastive/RLPO/balance are not reweighted. No claim that all loss terms have equal class mass.

For RETENTION/JOINT at s>0, snapshot its OWN previous-stage model, policy, exemplar bank, text, and prototypes before adding the new branch; teacher fully frozen. Old branch visual features of the student are exactly the old teacher branch outputs, so reuse those detached outputs; teacher experts/attention/prototypes stay fixed. No extra teacher image encoding. For an old replay row, q_old is teacher softmax over old classes. Extend q with zero probability for new classes. Retention is KL(q_extended || student softmax over ALL arrived classes), temperature1 in the existing score units. This penalizes stealing old-row probability into new classes, rather than merely preserving relative old-class logits. For each old row multiply by |T_s|/(n_old*N_sy), mean over the FULL microbatch; new rows contribute zero. Coefficient1 fixed. Expected objective is the mean across old classes of their retained-row KL. Stage0 retention is exactly zero, so BASE=RETENTION and MACRO=JOINT are expected paired controls at stage0. No retention parameter clipping/search/early stop. Teacher can preserve errors; this is a failure possibility, not an oracle target. The teacher depends only on previously trained parameters and retained TRAIN, never VAL.
