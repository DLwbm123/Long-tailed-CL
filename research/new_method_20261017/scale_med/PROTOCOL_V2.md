# V2 frozen protocol

One existing NIH author20 class order, seed 74002, six stages. Arms: full-768-CLS ridge; unshrunk uniform local Gaussian; shrunk uniform local Gaussian; ridge plus shrunk local scores. v1 statistics, projection, base reference and validation feature caches are reused read-only after matching configuration and completed status. Future-class statistics are loaded only at their arrival. Every stage must reproduce v1 ridge and uniform per-class accuracies.

Local scores are the uniform mean of shrunk Gaussian patch log likelihoods divided by 128, with no nonlinear reference mixture. Full ridge retains alpha=1 and patient/class-balanced sufficient statistics. Fusion is ridge/s_r + 0.5*local/s_l. Each scale is the square root of equal-base-class-average mean squared class-centered scores. At most 32 randomly selected patients per base class (fixed seed, sorted patient population), one lexicographically first image per patient, determine both scales using the fitted base classifiers. These are in-sample training scales, not accuracy calibration or a held-out calibration benchmark. No validation features or labels enter scale fitting. Both scales remain frozen at later stages. Zero scales fail rather than trigger retuning.

Budget: one run, at most 2 hours wall time from original start, no search, no additional seed/order, no test access. Only calibration needs fresh frozen-backbone inference; all remaining calculations use existing caches. DataLoader workers=0 avoids the prior multiprocessing NFS IPC cleanup issue. Completed calibration is atomic and reused after engineering repair. Prior logs and original deadline must be preserved.

Report final and mean-stage macro accuracy, fusion harm/rescue against ridge, local old-score drift, and new-class wins. Only local scores are invariant: the updated ridge and fusion need not be. Compare against the strong ridge control without claiming stability alone implies superiority. This is a follow-up on an already inspected development set.

Launch run_v2.py with RUN_CONFIG in the environment. Public config_v2.json additionally needs private source_run and output paths. Generate report_v2.py after COMPLETE. Public output allowlist: results.json, calibration.json (only scales/counts), summary.csv, REPORT.md. No private files, features, model checkpoints, patient identities or raw logs.
