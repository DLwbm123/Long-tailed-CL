## Long-tail / known-group extension

Use one mean CLIP embedding per known support group as the statistical observation, retaining original class/group/image target mean; no deletion of the original F loss or changed F training. This is a known-group variant of DEFTS, not a literal balanced-image-shot reproduction or patient-iid claim. Let G_c be support group count, u_gc the mean of unit image features inside group, and m_c their equal-group mean. All observations and estimates are support-only; query groups leave every producer statistic.

Center normalized class text vectors, retain smallest rank r explaining95% text scatter as in paper. Q is text-only. b_gc=Q^T u_gc, e_c=Q^T t_c, bbar_c=mean_g b_gc. Let nu=sum_c(G_c-1)>0 and S=sum_c,g(b_gc-bbar_c)(...)^T. Sigmahat=S/nu; sigma2=tr(Sigmahat)/r. Estimate tau2=(1/(Cr))*sum_c ||bbar_c-e_c||² - sigma2*mean_c(1/G_c). Precision Lambda=.1*(nu+2r+1)*(S+tr(Sigmahat)I)^(-1), paper beta=.1 fixed. Posterior theta_c=(I/tau2+G_c Lambda)^(-1)*(e_c/tau2+G_c Lambda*bbar_c). Singleton classes contribute zero within-class df but use covariance pooled from support classes with multiple known groups; do not pretend to estimate singleton-specific variance. If all G_c=1, tau2<=0, r=0, nonfinite, SPD/norm failure, mark UNRESOLVED and stop; no arbitrary10/90 split, clamp, added jitter or retry. Conditional Gaussian algebra does not certify actual residual model, semantic prior or clinical safety. Image risk remains individual-image BA, not group-centroid risk.
