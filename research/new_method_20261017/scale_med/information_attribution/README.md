Seven frozen arms distinguish global readout, a second global path, patch mean, mean quadratic features and within-image directional variance. This is an exposed first-transition benchmark, not independent blind validation or a new algorithm.

- [Final analysis and stop decision](matched_results/ANALYSIS_ZH.md)

No consistent second-order gain over the equal-dimensional control was established. Matching the penalty changed the secondary HK global-path comparison from negative to positive, demonstrating regularization sensitivity; patch mean remained inconclusive. No further local gates, seeds or projection dimensions were run. Historical exposure, tiny HK classes and unknown patient isolation limit all conclusions.

Private raw images, identities, per-image features/predictions and fitted heads are excluded.
